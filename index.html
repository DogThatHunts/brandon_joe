  • Core Worldview

    Brandon Joe's core legal framework, organized by topic:

    1. Two citizenships — "state citizenship" (reserved, in his telling, for free white persons who are citizens of one of the 50 states) is wholly separate from "U.S. citizenship," which he says Congress created via the Civil Rights Act of 1866/14th Amendment to protect freed slaves; state citizens, in his view, have no rights or obligations under the 14th Amendment.
    2. The sole proprietorship / strawman — every person born in the U.S. has a second, separate "person" (a sole proprietorship) born alongside them, evidenced by the birth certificate; that sole proprietorship — not the living man — is the actual "U.S. citizen," "taxpayer," and party to commercial transactions.
    3. UCC Article 3 and "infinite money" — every bill, Federal Reserve note, and credit-card transaction is a "negotiable instrument" (never "money"), which he says can be endorsed and tendered back as full payment, and used via a credit union's Federal Reserve discount-window access to generate what he calls "infinite money."
    4. Taxes and "taxpayer" — "taxpayer" status is an unproven IRS presumption; "United States"/"State" in federal statutes mean only D.C. and the territories; income-tax liability attaches only to officers/employees of the federal government.
    5. Foreign government / nation-building — any individual or group can lawfully constitute itself as a "foreign government" under 18 U.S.C. 11 merely by being "at peace," modeled on his own self-declared "Nation of the Amnesty Coalition."
    6. Driving vs. traveling — driving/travel is covered by the 9th/10th Amendments for state citizens; the Commerce Clause is the only route the federal government has to reach it, and only for commercial hauling.
    7. Litigation strategy — an elaborate, self-taught discovery/UCC/evidence-code litigation framework built around his own ongoing "City of Glendale" case, including a template he says lets a non-attorney represent others in court.
    8. Race and the 14th Amendment — his stated life goal is repeal of the 14th Amendment, paired with an explicitly racialized vision of post-repeal citizenship admission.

    Flagged contradictions (4 total) — the document's most useful critical content — include: race treated as both a hard legal line and a gameable, unenforceable presumption; the commercial/ non-commercial distinction treated as both decisive and irrelevant; a "race-neutral legal technicality" framing alongside an explicitly racial post-repeal sorting scheme in the same video; and repeated self-correction of prior course material ("all horse shit") without acknowledging the same structural pattern in what he currently recommends.
