# Research Timeline

  1. Had a landscaping company and was being attacked by the Employment Development Dept (hereafter “EDD”) in the middle of 2021. They came onto a jobsite, started interrogating the employees, then sent me a bill for maybe $60,000 (in a company which wasn’t really turning a profit yet because I got the company for free because it was a burning dumpster of shit.)

  2. At this time, for my day job, I had been the office manager for a very high-end dental office in Encino, California since 2015 but I only worked 3 days a week. So that left lots of time to do research.

  3. I had a client at the time named Nancy Kremer, who ran the Los Angeles Assembly under Anna Von Reitz. She had invited me to come over to her house for around 2 years to tell me about some of this sovereignty and legal stuff but I was too scared. Once the EDD finally came after me, my business partner Tracy Baron pushed me to go see her. He came with. We went specifically to try and learn more about how to handle this EDD issue. This was in maybe Sept of 2021.

  4. I was terrified but I did the papers she had: notarizing them, etc. I believe the term was still “state national” back them. I believe it was called like a “928” pack or something with a handful of documents. Seems like a lifetime ago. I remember being so scared that I was literally hot, dizzy and seeing double. I thought the government was going to come busting in through the windows any second, but I did it anyways.

  5. After leaving Nancy’s house, I decided I wanted to go and study the subject on my own… alone. So I began to try to look for information online but couldn’t find much. The occasional rantings and ravings of someone on a website that looked like it was from 1993 was about all that was available in Sept 2021. But I went on Amazon and found a ton of books on the subject. I bought maybe 10 books (which quickly turned into over 26 books) and began reading.

  6. There was no way to validate the information but it was very shocking. I ready those 25-30 books from Sept 2021 through March 2022. As I was reading these, I decided I wanted to make a free online resource diluting down all the books I was reading.

  7. I got back from a long trip in early March 2022. I already had my passport, revocation of election, trusts, Federal trademark of my name, name change and several other processes all ready to go and I wanted to do them all immediately right when I got home. So the first week I submitted by passport, sent in my revocation of election and did every other process I could possibly get my hands on. At this time I was working with a man here in the Los Angeles area named John Woodruff. He was the one helping me put all this stuff together and I was referred to him by Nancy Kremer.

  8. I already had the domain name onestupidfuck.com because I have been a professional marketing person most of my adult life and I always knew I wanted to start some kind of personal brand using it but just didn’t know on what subject. This is when I knew I found how to use this URL. Onestupidfuck.com was born on March 14th, 2022 and the prime page on it, at the time, was called the “state national theory” page. I had actually been working on writing the state national theory page since January so it was a long-time coming for me to release it.

  9. Shortly after making my first trust and also launching this website, I moved all the assets, titles, etc, from the landscaping company over to the brand new trust that I had just made. Then I promptly told the EDD to go fuck themselves and told them that everything was now in a foreign private trust. Shockingly, they did! This was my very first huge win. This was probably right around mid or late March 2022.

  10. Very little traffic was generated to this site for March-May 2022, but the thing that everyone kept saying they wanted was some kind of video presentation to show the material more. I kept insisting no, but finally caved and began shooting the videos for the 1.0 Contract Killer Course (hereafter “CKC”) in May of 2022 and shot non-stop through the end of June, then the 1.0 CKC was launched on July 2nd, 2022. 1.0 CKC was just videos of me covering the state national theory page, point-by-point and is still available on my YouTube channel (even though I don’t recommend you watch it unless you have an amazing grasp of all the newest info and just want a little trip down memory lane).

  11. Traffic was through the roof almost immediately. June of 2022 was 535 visits to this site, July was 3598. The rocket had taken off and people loved the 1.0 CKC.

  12. By August 3rd of 2022, over 1800 people had signed up for the 1.0 CKC.

  13. Shortly after the launch of 1.0, the branding was selected by the fans. There was a part in 1.0 where I said “if you don’t understand this stuff, just head down to your local farmer’s market and find yourself the biggest, fattest cucumber you can find and then sit on it you stupid fuck.” This was a massive winner by fan choice and so I made that the entire branding of this website. That, combined with the classic mankini, became the branding for my entire platform. I believe the branding was completed in August of 2022. The merch store was launched right after the branding was completed.

  14. A friend of mine had a podcasting PR company that got you on podcasts. I bought his largest package, which was I think 48 shows in 12 months. Shows began and I was doing more than one per week and this only accelerated as time progressed.

  15. In Sept of 2022, I had around $177,000 on a business AMEX card for both my own stuff and the landscaping stuff. We became unable to pay it so I decided to just let it crash. Once it was sent to collections in November, I wrote some letters to the collections company and I got a letter from their law firm lettering me know they would not be pursuing the debt (you can see this letter if you scroll to the bottom of the [CKC signup page](/contractkillercourse)). This was my second huge win.

  16. By the end of 2022, we had 11,298 people coming to this website in just December alone.

  17. 2022 was filled with the typical old-school shit that we used to do back in the day: the revocation of election, passports, 1099a forms, etc. I continued to research, continued to update the state national theory page and continued to release new post-course videos to the 1.0 CKC as time progressed.

  18. 2023 was the year of non-stop shows. I believe most of the year I was doing 2-5 shows a week for most of the year. Traffic started in Jan at 16,318 and grew to 46,984 in December.

  19. 2023 was the year when I began a fun side project helping people get free cars by indorsing the original documentation using a qualified indorsement (“without recourse”). Many dozens of people got cars and if you scroll on my Instagram back to this time period you will find tons of posts of people getting free cars. This is still possible and occasionally someone does it but I moved on from that project in late 2023.

  20. Most of 2023 was spent learning about negotiable instruments and financials. Heavy research into that subject consumed most of that year.

  21. Williams and WILLIAMS Law Firm (hereafter “W&W”) website was launched in August 2023 but it had no real information on it until later. It was only used to invite clients. It did not become very content-heavy until 2025. It was getting around 7,000 visitors per month through 2023.

  22. The original 1.0 CKC was actually on a platform called Vimeo. By Sept of 2023 we had out-grown Vimeo and we were forced off the platform. I got a call from their team and they said we were consuming more than 14 terabytes of bandwidth monthly, which they had never really seen. At this time is when all the videos and the course was moved to YouTube.

  23. Right around Sept/Oct of 2023 is when I left my day job at the dental office. My obsession for this legal work had fully gripped me even several months before this point and myself and the Doctor knew, by this point, that it was simply unsustainable. I had become entirely engrossed by this research and just honestly didn’t want to do anything else with my time every single day. This was when I went full-time into my platform.

  24. Right around November 2023 is when I realized that I had exhausted all avenues for “administrative relief.” I knew that I needed to learn other ways to enforce what was being sent in. I had tried Polaroids and all sorts of goofy stuff and I had done a billion processes by this point and there was rarely any response to anything. This is when I finally took a deep breath and realized that litigation is the only path forward.

  25. I attempted to find anyone that could teach me litigation and ended up getting scammed by this guy who I paid to help me learn. I ended up making an email blast warning everyone about this scammer and Joey Kimbrough actually responded to that email. He was on my email list and had signed up for the 1.0 CKC.

  26. After about a month of me going back and forth with Joey and looking over all his cases, I decided we should start working together and we began our super-duo in December of 2023.

  27. 2024 was the year of litigation. I also booked my first in-person speaking gig at Confluence in Bandera, Texas. The guy who hooked me up with that was Alec Zeck (<https://thewayfwrd.com/>). The in-person event was in April of 2024.

  28. 2024 was the peak traffic. This website was getting about 75,000 visitors per month. Litigation was being filed all throughout this year. I thought litigation was going to be like taking candy from a baby, but instead I ended up getting my face smashed in essentially the entire year. But we were learning at a blisteringly-fast rate.

  29. Via Alec Zeck, I set up a whole circuit of in-person shows in the Austin area prior to speaking at Confluence (<https://www.confluenceevent.com/>). So I arrived in Austin in March of 2024 and spent that whole month just exploring Austin, being introduced to tons of people in the sovereignty and freedom movement there and doing big shows such as The Great Unlearn with Cal Callahan, The Life Stylist Podcast with Luke Storey, The Way Forward Podcast with Alec Zeck, the Kyle Kingsbury Podcast, the Conservatish Podcast with Peter Feliciano, etc. Then right after all that is when I spoke at Confluence in Bandera Texas.

  30. The Amnesty Coalition was hosted here on this page until it was given its own website in May of 2024.

  31. I began shooting new video to update all the research in July of 2024, then the 2.0 CKC was released on 8/18/24. This was a MASSIVE improvement from 1.0 and you could see that the frame of the actual technology of all this information began to actually take shape. The release of 2.0 was the absolute peak of interest and traffic onto my platform. 2.0 is still available on my YouTube channel but I recommend you only view it if you understand the newest information cold and just want to take a trip down memory lane.

  32. I was still doing many shows in 2024.

  33. In November of 2024 is when I came to the massive breakthrough regarding the sole proprietorship and shifted everything over to that right away moving into 2025.

  34. 2025 is when things began to change. I decided to step away from litigation and regroup because I was losing so badly in the Courts and I knew I was obviously missing the mark dramatically. I began reading tons of cases and trying to understand the case law and the court procedure. The Treatise on the Word Person was something I started writing in early April of 2025 because of this. This is when the platform began to shift over to much of the racial stuff that you see today. The more cases I read, the more I realized that all of American law is solely based on race. Prior to this point my platform had NOTHING involving race but it also had false case law quotes on the various websites and other trash. I realized right around the beginning of 2025 that a majority of the “case law” shared in the sovereignty circles is entirely false. I began a journey to clean all that up and my love for reading case law was born. This also resulted in the Case Law Resource page on W&W.

  35. Shortly after I began working on the Treatise, I had my second in-person speaking event but this one was headlined by me, Joey Kimbrough and Cameron Wilson. Video recordings of this are available on my YouTube channel. This was actually a full 3-day event in Austin, Texas.

  36. In May of 2025 I was invited to be a speaker at Dave Asprey’s Biohacking Conference in Austin, Texas. That was a blast. I spoke about negotiable instruments. That is a very prestigious event and I was pretty much the only person it seemed like who was invited to speak who was not a health guru, which I considered a compliment of the highest magnitude.

  37. The Code of a Pickletarian was written in July of 2025 after I saw the movie Sovereign. I wanted to make sure that something like that doesn’t happen within our group.

  38. Later in 2025, I wrote my book, _The Guide to the Most Beautiful Woman in the World: The United States of America_. This was in October of 2025.

  39. Right around Oct/Nov of 2025 is also when I submitted my very first submission to the US Supreme Court via a writ of certiorari. But it took 5 months for them to evaluate it so it wasn’t actually docketed until 2026.

  40. 2025 traffic statistics of traffic to each website was 148,000 for The Amnesty Coalition, 306,000 for W&W and 389,000 for One Stupid Fuck. YouTube channel watch hours came down from 667,000 in 2025 to 481,000 in 2026.

  41. I was doing shows and working hard with research all through 2025 but sort of crashed after finishing my book and my writ of certiorari. I was working all day every day for months and ended up taking the last 2 months or so of 2025 off for my own health and sanity.

  42. 2026 started with some litigation but I already knew I wanted to make the 3.0 version of the CKC because I knew it was time. But it required me to basically go through every single page of all three websites and also go through all the template documents available on The Amnesty Coalition website. This took me months. I began shooting the videos in April, after a tremendous amount of prep work was completed. The prep work was almost daily from mid Jan until I began shooting the videos in April of 2026. Then the 3.0 CKC was released on 5/16/26. During the prep work of making 3.0, I retired the original page that started my entire platform: the “state national theory” page (which later became the “State citizen theory” page once I realized there was no such thing as a “state national”).

  43. My first writ of certiorari was finally docketed in April of 2026. This was the point when I went through almost every aspect of my websites and documents and cleaned everything up and polished all my court filings to look much more “professional.” This was the point where I knew we were now in the big leagues because less than half a percent of any attorney ever files a single writ of certiorari in their entire careers.

  44. Due to the new very heavy racial focus, as well as a new total focus on litigation in the 3.0 CKC, view numbers were down dramatically and there was a sizable fall-off of interest and traffic to both my websites and the YouTube videos. This is understandable because the truth of American law is a gruesome sight if you are not willing to view racism. Also, many people express a desire to attempt to avoid litigation. While this is understandable, it is a complete waste of time. I spent thousands of hours trying to avoid litigation to no avail. So I will continue to blaze this path hoping that people will come around. We have many great people filing and moving forward with effective litigation and I hope to see many more as soon as they have realized that they are wasting their time with other efforts.

  45. Mainline research was essentially complete once I filed my first amended complaint into the City of Glendale California Superior Court case (case #13 on W&W) and the originating complaint in the Federal case (case #15 on W&W). This was the exact point when I knew we had completed a 5-year long very intense research project spanning over 15,000 hours of work on my part. Not to say there may not be realizations or breakthroughs here or there, but I knew my focus every single day of the week on litigation and being locked away digging through definitions and cases was officially over. Each and every element had been carefully paved and detailed, beyond anything that has ever been available before. These are the first two case filings where I was able to successfully separate out myself as a white citizen of California and my sole proprietorship with exact and specific causes of action for both plaintiffs. Prior to this point I had never stepped into the courts myself, as a white Californian, because I didn’t know how. I only had the sole proprietorship enter the court. It took me years of work to understand and feel comfortable actually entering the court as a white Californian.

  46. The shift from the research era to the community era of my platform began in October 2026 with the announcement of a Skool group which will be launched in December of 2026. This will be the Pickletarian War Room and will be the beginning of organized APPLICATION of all of my research. It will be a war and strategy room, not a research room. And the definition of “war” means a war on ignorance, the 14th Amendment and negotiable instruments being used as currency in violation of Article I, Section 10 of the US Constitution. This doesn’t mean a war “against the government” or some other trash that has nothing to do with what we stand for.
