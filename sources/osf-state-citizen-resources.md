#### How to get copies of your passport documentation once you use the Amnesty Coalition explanatory statement:

a. Write an affidavit requesting certified copies of the passport that you applied for on behalf of the sole prop. This must be notarized as the agent/owner of the sole prop.

b. Include your full name of the sole prop from its birth and any name changes you have given him, date and place of sole prop birth, current mailing address, daytime phone number, current email address, date passport was issued (info in your BOOK), passport number, photo of passport card or driver’s license.

c. Check for $50 per set made out to: Dept of State

d. Mailed to:

US Dept of State  
Office of Law Enforcement Liaison Division  
44132 Mercure Circle  
PO Box 1227  
Sterling, VA 20166

This final packet that you will get will have a Dept of State cover letter with a gold-embossed seal and will have a gold ring that will bind that cover letter to your original DS-11 along with all notations on the bottom by the workers who process it, as well as a computerized printout of your computer profile on their end.
