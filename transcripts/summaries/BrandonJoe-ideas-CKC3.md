# Brandon Joe Williams' Legal Ideas, by Topic

Summary of Brandon Joe Williams' legal claims and arguments across all 16 transcripts of his
"Civilian Knowledge Course 3.0" (CKC 3.0) video series plus two related "City of Glendale Round 3"
litigation case-breakdown videos (source: `transcripts/TXT/01`–`16`). Brandon Joe is the sole
speaker throughout — no host, unlike the KL_on_Crrow/KL_on_RiceRadio projects these notes are
modeled on. Organized by topic rather than chronologically. This is a summary of what he said,
not a legal assessment — citations are not verified here (see `citations-sidecar.md` for that
pass). Contradictions are flagged inline with `> **Contradiction:**` blockquotes and rolled up at
the end.

## Citizenship and legal-fiction theory

**Claim:** America's foundational citizenship class is "state citizenship" — reserved for free
white persons who are citizens of one of the 50 states of the Union — and is entirely separate
from "U.S. citizenship" / "citizen of the United States," a class he says Congress created by the
Civil Rights Act of 1866 (later folded into the 14th Amendment) specifically to protect freed
slaves from Southern "Black Codes." He argues the 14th Amendment, the incorporation doctrine, and
the entire U.S.-citizen apparatus have nothing to do with state citizens, and that white citizens
of a state have no rights *or obligations* under the 14th Amendment.

- **Support:** Dred Scott v. Sandford (1857); Civil Rights Act of 1866; Slaughterhouse Cases
  (1873); Bradwell v. State (1873); Ex parte Knowles (Cal. 1855); Van Valkenburgh v. Brown (1872);
  U.S. v. Cruikshank (1876); Downes v. Bidwell (1901); Twining v. New Jersey; Strauder v. West
  Virginia (1880); Madden v. [Kentucky, misstated as] "Commissioner" (1940); Jones v. Temmer
  (1993); Hepburn & Dundas v. Ellzey (1805, John Marshall opinion).
- **Source:** [01](../TXT/01_20260806_CKC 3.0 Post Course Video 1 - Practising Law Without a License and First Amended_0bec3cba.txt), "I was born a white citizen of Indiana and I'm currently a white citizen of California"; [02](../TXT/02_20260806_CKC 3.0 - Video 1 - Citizenship for Men_ Women and Children _not legal fictions__84335cec.txt), "sovereign citizen was the correct term the whole time"; [03](../TXT/03_20260806_CKC 3.0 - Video 2 - Legal Fictions and the Sole Proprietorship_f75ddbab.txt), "it's all Dred Scott ... everything is Dred Scott versus Sanford everywhere you go."

**Claim:** The 9th Amendment is "the sovereignty amendment" and is the single biggest practical
right a white state citizen has — it covers every license-requiring activity (driving, fishing,
practicing law or medicine) without needing a license, because licensing exists only to grant
14th-Amendment persons privileges they don't otherwise have.

- **Support:** James Madison's June 8, 1789 remarks on the proposed 9th Amendment; the fact that
  the 9th and 10th Amendments have "never been incorporated" into the 14th Amendment class.
- **Source:** [02](../TXT/02_...84335cec.txt), "The Ninth Amendment is the sovereignty amendment... Doesn't apply to non-whites"; [03](../TXT/03_...f75ddbab.txt), "every license under the Sun, Moon and Stars would all be under the Ninth Amendment."

## Sole proprietorship, trusts, and legal/beneficial ownership

**Claim:** The W-9 form itself proves the sole-proprietorship theory: it requires an "owner" line
separate from the "business/disregarded entity" line, and requires the signer to certify they are
a "U.S. person" — something a white state citizen cannot truthfully sign without committing
perjury.

- **Source:** [03](../TXT/03_...f75ddbab.txt), "Enter the owner's name on line one and enter the business slash disregarded entity's name on line two."

**Claim:** Every item of personal property (cars, trusts) splits into "legal ownership" (the
trustee/title-holder) and "beneficial ownership" (the one who enjoys the economic use), and
certificates (silver certificates, certificates of title, certificates of trust) are, as a
category, never the thing itself but only an "abstract" proving the real asset/title exists
somewhere else — so a DMV certificate of title proves a legal-title/beneficial-title split exists
between the registered owner and the state, the precise mechanics of which (who holds legal title,
who holds beneficial title) he says must be established through discovery because he and his main
collaborator, Joe Lustig(ka), disagree about which side is which.

- **Source:** [07](../TXT/07_...0f44cef8.txt), "What's a certificate of title? It means the title is someplace else"; [08](../TXT/08_...72664d37.txt), "Joe Lustica thinks that when you go to the DMV you are actually becoming the legal owner and the state is becoming the beneficial owner. I'm not sure."

**Claim:** A trust indenture is a purely private contract that never needs to be shown to a bank;
only a one-page "certificate of trust" needs to be produced, and (he says, as of 2024–2025) major
banks now have their own certificate-of-trust templates that require only the trust's own EIN, not
the settlor's SSN — which he treats as a direct, unacknowledged concession to pressure from the
sovereignty movement.

- **Source:** [07](../TXT/07_...0f44cef8.txt), "within probably about 60 days of me releasing this template ... all these banks started to make their own certificate of trust template."

## UCC Article 3, negotiable instruments, and "infinite money"

**Claim:** Every bill, Federal Reserve note, check, and credit-card transaction is legally a
"negotiable instrument" under UCC/CCC Article 3 — never "money" (which under Article I §10 and
31 U.S.C. 5103 must be gold or silver) — and a negotiable instrument need only satisfy one live
element (UCC 3-104(a)(3), "unconditional") because the other two statutory elements
(payable-to-bearer-or-order, and payable-on-demand-or-at-a-definite-time) default to "yes"
whenever the instrument is silent on them (UCC 3-205(b); UCC 3-108(a)). He calls this "the most
important aspect of this entire flowchart" and the basis of all his litigation.

- **Source:** [05](../TXT/05_...5e25ad83.txt), "since number one and number two have a built-in default, this point number three is actually the only thing the instrument actually needs to have"; [11](../TXT/11_...7b052aac.txt), same point repeated as the centerpiece of his discovery requests.

**Claim:** Because a bill (an "unconditional order to pay") has the same legal status as a
Federal Reserve note, a recipient can endorse the original bill ("without recourse, pay to the
order of bearer" or back to the issuer) and tender it back as full payment under CCC/UCC 3-311
("accord and satisfaction by use of instrument"); the drawee has no authority under the UCC to
selectively refuse a properly-endorsed instrument (CCC 3-603(b)), and California Evidence Code
§§633–634 create presumptions ("an obligation delivered up to the debtor is presumed to have
been paid"; "a person in possession of an order on himself ... is presumed to have paid the money")
that he says shift the burden of proof onto the creditor.

- **Source:** [10](../TXT/10_...8b74a13b.txt), "The drawer does not have the ability to selectively choose what kinds of notes or drafts he would like to accept"; [13](../TXT/13_...41cbe397.txt), "An obligation delivered up to the debtor ... is presumed to have been paid. What in the fuck?"

**Claim:** Anyone (with six friends) can open a federal credit union, get OC-10 Federal Reserve
discount-window access, and then swap any negotiable instrument — including self-manufactured
promissory notes or other people's unpaid bills — for actual Federal Reserve notes under 12 U.S.C.
412, "without recourse," so the credit union (not the original obligor) bears any loss. He
describes this as the mechanism of "infinite money" and offers to personally help the first three
groups that open a federal credit union.

- **Source:** [05](../TXT/05_...5e25ad83.txt), "Without recourse, paid to the order of Quicken Loans, Inc. ... I never have to pay back the Federal Reserve, but the customer now owes me for 30 years on their mortgage."

**Claim:** The Federal Reserve Banks are "not federal instrumentalities" but "independent,
privately owned, and locally controlled corporations," per John L. Lewis v. United States (9th
Cir. 1982, Judge Cecil Poole).

- **Source:** [05](../TXT/05_...5e25ad83.txt); [10](../TXT/10_...8b74a13b.txt) (repeated as background for the Glendale litigation).

> **Contradiction:** He repeatedly says "I don't think I've ever denied" that the private motor
> carrier DOT registration and interstate commerce are inherently commercial activities
> ([08](../TXT/08_...72664d37.txt)), yet the entire thrust of his "driving vs. traveling" argument
> is that *commercial vs. non-commercial* is "mostly irrelevant" for anyone under the 14th

> concedes is commercial in nature would matter at all to someone he says the Commerce Clause

## Taxes, "taxpayer," and IRS procedure

**Claim:** The word "taxpayer" (26 U.S.C. 7701(a)(14), "any person subject to any internal revenue
tax") is the IRS's central legal fiction/presumption — virtually every IRS form and the entire
"Frivolous Tax Positions" page uses the word "taxpayer" as an unproven presumption, and simply
denying taxpayer status (while never affirmatively claiming it) is, in his view, sufficient
defense. He also argues "trade or business" (26 U.S.C. 7701(a)(26)) "includes the performance of
the functions of a public office," and that "employee" (26 U.S.C. 3401(c)) is limited to officers/
employees of the District of Columbia or federal instrumentalities — so income-tax withholding
and liability apply only to persons working in or for the federal government.

- **Source:** [06](../TXT/06_...289300b9.txt), "The word taxpayer, you have to actually go into the actual arguments ... it's 107 times. That's the secret. That's the linchpin."

**Claim:** "United States" and "State," wherever defined in federal statutes (26 U.S.C. 7701(a)(9)
-(10); 18 U.S.C. 5; 4 U.S.C. 71-72), mean only the District of Columbia (plus U.S. territories/
insular possessions) — never the 50 states of the Union — and 26 U.S.C. 7701(a)(39) ("such
citizen or resident shall be treated as residing in the District of Columbia") is, in his view,
proof that every U.S. citizen's/sole proprietorship's legal domicile is permanently the District
of Columbia regardless of physical location.

- **Source:** [06](../TXT/06_...289300b9.txt); repeated extensively in [10](../TXT/10_...8b74a13b.txt) and [11](../TXT/11_...7b052aac.txt) as the basis for his litigation's jurisdictional theory.

**Claim:** He abandoned his earlier ("2.0 course") advice to file a "revocation of election"
document, calling it "all horse shit" once he understood 26 U.S.C. 7701(a)(39) — because there is
no "election" to be treated as residing in D.C.; it is a non-optional statutory presumption for
anyone who is a U.S. citizen/resident, so there is nothing to revoke.

- **Source:** [06](../TXT/06_...289300b9.txt), "I had my own revocation of election document ... I had to throw it out the fucking nearest window."

**Claim:** An IRS bill can be made to legally "vanish" by endorsing it back to the IRS ("I hereby
authorize the campus to settle the account of the taxpayer ... using the draft ... sent by using
Fedwire") citing an unspecified IRM provision, combined with a W-8EXP filed on behalf of a
self-created "foreign government" (per 18 U.S.C. 11 and 26 U.S.C. 892) rather than the earlier
W-8BEN approach he now disfavors because of its treaty-benefits section.

## Driving versus traveling, licensing, and jurisdiction

**Claim:** The right to drive/travel is covered entirely by the 9th/10th Amendments for white
state citizens (no case law needed), and the Commerce Clause (Art. I §8 cl.3) is the *only* route
by which the federal government can reach a white state citizen's driving at all — and even then
only for interstate commercial hauling. For 14th-Amendment persons, by contrast, the Commerce
Clause is irrelevant because 26 U.S.C. 7701(a)(39) gives Congress plenary jurisdiction regardless
of where they physically are.

- **Source:** [08](../TXT/08_...72664d37.txt), "the federal government always has plenary power over 14th amendment citizens always always always... that's what we saw on 26 USC 7701 subsection a 39."

**Claim:** A California driver's license creates "a rebuttable presumption that the owner is a
resident of California" (Cal. Vehicle Code §4000(h)), which he treats as per se incompatible with
being a (non-resident) white citizen of a state — hence his rule that no "ambassador" of his
nation may hold a driver's license, and his own practice of carrying a "private motor carrier"
DOT registration (a free FMCSA number) instead, on the theory that a private carrier (unlike a
"common carrier") does not carry a built-in presumption of commercial activity that the state
must otherwise disprove.

- **Source:** [08](../TXT/08_...72664d37.txt), full discussion, including his own two incidents of having utility power shut off and restored same day.

**Claim:** Jury pools in California are built from DMV, Franchise Tax Board, and voter-registration
records of state tax filers, drivers, and registered voters (Cal. Civ. Proc. Code §203, §197) —
all explicitly limited to "citizens of the United States" — which he treats as proof that
non-driving, non-voting, non-tax-filing white state citizens cannot be compelled into that jury
pool, and that if compelled to serve he would demand a jury limited to "white citizens of
California who are living in the Glendale area."

- **Source:** [12](../TXT/12_...c03e4b61.txt).

## Litigation procedure, discovery, and presumptions

**Claim:** "There is no such thing as an administrative process" — every pre-suit communication
(letters, calls) should be understood only as evidence-gathering for an eventual complaint,
because opposing parties "would rather die" than substantively respond, and the entire value of
his platform is in teaching people to get into discovery as fast as possible, where (he argues)
the balance of power flips because the other side must answer questions under oath they would
otherwise never discuss (what a bill's routing numbers mean, where remittance stubs are
processed, who is the legal/beneficial owner of a car, etc.).

- **Source:** [09](../TXT/09_...81771878.txt), "there is no such thing as an administrative process ... it's just preparation for litigation."

**Claim:** Every cause of action reduces to duty → breach → damages, and the fastest, lowest-risk
way to manufacture a first lawsuit is to intentionally under-pay (or otherwise trigger late fees
from) a utility company, because the resulting multi-hour power shutoff supplies "damages" without
meaningful real-world cost, and the entire cycle (bill → endorse and tender → shutoff → sue) can
be repeated indefinitely to build successive "rounds" of litigation (he describes already
preparing a "Round 4" complaint against the City of Glendale while "Round 3" is still pending).

- **Source:** [09](../TXT/09_...81771878.txt); [12](../TXT/12_...c03e4b61.txt); [16](../TXT/16_...991a78e2.txt).

**Claim:** A plaintiff should file an "unlimited" civil case (>$35,000 demanded) rather than a
"limited" case (≤$35,000), because — per his own reading, cross-checked with AI — a limited case's
appeal routes only to an internal appellate division of the Superior Court, is harder to carry to
the Court of Appeal, and functions as a near-irreversible waiver of the right eventually to reach
the state Supreme Court; he set his own Glendale complaint at exactly $35,001 for this reason.

- **Source:** [10](../TXT/10_...8b74a13b.txt), "you're telling me if I do a limited case I can't even get into the court of appeals ... I'm not waiving my right to go to the supreme court."

**Claim:** Under California Rules of Civil Procedure, a demand for discovery responses by mail
carries an extra five days to respond, but delivery by process server does not — a distinction
his own opposing counsel allegedly missed, causing a tentative ruling in his favor at a motion-to-
compel hearing; separately, he learned (from the same hearing) that a single motion to compel
covering requests for admission, interrogatories, and document requests together is procedurally
defective — each discovery type requires its own motion, with its own reservation number and
filing fee.

- **Source:** [12](../TXT/12_...c03e4b61.txt), full tentative-order walkthrough.

**Claim:** Under California Evidence Code §402/"evidentiary hearing" practice, an opposing party's
evidence can be defeated in two separate stages — first by attacking the "preliminary facts" a
presumption is built on (a §402 hearing), then, only if that fails, by attacking the "proffered
evidence" itself (an evidentiary hearing) — giving a litigant "two times up to bat" if the
sequence is used correctly rather than jumping straight to an evidentiary hearing.

- **Source:** [13](../TXT/13_...41cbe397.txt).

> **Flagged inconsistency (not a contradiction, but worth noting):** He states in file

## Contracts, consideration, and the "practice law without a license" template

**Claim:** A contract requires only consideration ("something of value in the eye of the law") and
an agreement to do or not do something; a bill itself constitutes consideration/cash the moment it
creates a debt obligation between two parties, regardless of whether the recipient subjectively
regards it as valuable. Credit-card "1% cash back" is, in his view, legally inadequate
consideration because the issuer captures ~99% of the instrument's value.

- **Source:** [07](../TXT/07_...0f44cef8.txt).

**Claim:** A "template to practice law without a license," built from an online legal-treatise
resource (citing, per his account, three unnamed cases on "the right to practice law in the state
courts," plus Corpus Juris Secundum §2 and 8 U.S.C. 1101(a)(22)(A)/1401(a)/1101(b)(3)), lets a
white state citizen represent other people or legal fictions in court — including class actions —
without a bar license, because bar-licensing requirements derive from the 14th Amendment's
privileges-and-immunities clause and therefore bind only 14th-Amendment persons. He filed his own
amended complaint splitting himself (the natural person/plaintiff) from his sole proprietorship
(a separate plaintiff) on this theory, which he says was accepted for docketing at the U.S.
Supreme Court (No. 25-1221) without triggering Supreme Court Rule 9.1 (non-attorney representation
of another party).

- **Source:** [01](../TXT/01_...0bec3cba.txt), full template walkthrough.

1. **Race as legal fact vs. unenforceable presumption.** The 9th Amendment / state citizenship is
   described as categorically unavailable to non-whites ([02](../TXT/02_...84335cec.txt)), yet
   elsewhere he concedes a non-white can simply assert whiteness on official forms because "the

   distinction is the entire basis of the Commerce Clause argument for state citizens, but is
   called "mostly irrelevant" for everyone else, including for the very private-motor-carrier
   registration he recommends, which he concedes is "ultimately ... commercial in nature"
   ([08](../TXT/08_...72664d37.txt)).
3. **Race-neutral structure vs. racial sorting mechanism.** The state-citizen/14th-Amendment split
   is described in the same video both as a race-neutral legal/historical fact and as the basis
   for a proposed post-repeal regime with explicitly racial admission criteria and consequences
   ([16](../TXT/16_...991a78e2.txt)).
4. **Self-correction without acknowledging prior error's scope.** He repeatedly announces that
   prior courses (1.0, 2.0) and his own earlier advice (four- and five-star passports, "revocation
   of election," "non-citizen national" status, calling himself a "sovereign citizen" an oxymoron)
   were "all horse shit," while continuing to recommend some structurally similar devices (e.g.,
   self-naturalizing trusts into foreign jurisdictions at will) without flagging the parallel.
