Status key: Resolved (URL built from a register resolver or a well-established public citation), Unresolved (no resolver applies and no reliable public citation could be confirmed from the transcript alone — flagged for manual verification, no URL guessed).

5. California state statutes and rules (no resolver — flagged per project brief)

No category in the shared register’s schema currently models California Code of Civil Procedure, Evidence Code, or Rules of Court as a distinct citation type. These are listed here for human review on whether/how to extend the schema, consistent with extract_citations.md’s instruction to flag rather than invent a category unilaterally.

  • Cal. Civ. Proc. Code §§ 446, 1060, 197, 203, 283, 2025.010–2036.050 (discovery act), 395 — jurisdiction, verification, jury-pool eligibility, attorney authority, the Civil Discovery Act. (09, 10, 11, 12)
  • Cal. Evid. Code §§ 110–670 generally, incl. §§ 412, 500–502, 600–606, 633, 634, 637(?), 1152, 1400–1402 — burdens of proof/producing evidence, presumptions (conclusive and rebuttable), hearsay, authentication, settlement-communication privilege. (10, 11, 13)
  • Cal. Vehicle Code § 4000(h), § 6700 — rebuttable presumption of residency from a driver’s license; non-resident registration exemption. (08)
  • Cal. Pub. Util. Code § 2106 — his alternate jurisdictional basis in the Glendale complaint. (10)
  • Cal. Rules of Court, rule 2.100 et seq. — pleading-paper formatting requirements. (10)
  • Cal. Rules of Professional Conduct, rules 1.0.1, 3.1, 3.3, 3.4, 4.3, 5.1, 8.4 — “slave code” discussion; his bar-complaint strategy. (14)
  • California Constitution of 1849 and California Constitution of 1879 — treated as two parallel, still-active constitutions for two different citizenship classes. (01)
